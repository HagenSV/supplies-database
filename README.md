# Purpose

For several years my parents have managed church camps for our local presbytery. Their job has always been managing registrations and dividing campers into teams. On occasion, they also actually direct the camps, making them responsible for planning activities and sourcing materials for those activities. Since materials are paid for by the presbytery, they remain property of the presbytery. My parents hold on to them on behalf of the presbytery since they don't have a storage unit (yet).

Where we run into problems is that other prospective camp directors don't know what supplies we already have. While we could use simple spreadsheet to have a record of all our supplies, that would require a lot of manual data management (which is prone to errors) and searching it would be cumbersome.

This supplies database is just once piece of a larger puzzle to help improve our church camp management. With this program, not only can we have a convenient system to keep track of Presbytery supplies, we can also have a reservation system with item checkin and checkout with audit logs to know who last had an item when it goes missing.

Once the supplies database is complete, I would like to expand the application with more utilities.

My mom spends a lot of time carefully crafting teams to ensure that they are balanced in many ways, I would like to design an algorithm to to most of the heavy lifting.

I would like to create a system to automatically generate reports from Planning Center (our registration software) registrations.
Some reports we need include: a list of dietary restrictions for the camp kitchen staff, medication information for the nurse book, and other general reports.

Currently, my father manages them by downloading the registration data from Planning Center and formatting it in a word document with a mail merge.

The ultimate goal of this project is to both save my parents some time and to simplify the management of church camps. While my parents handle it very well, they wont be able to do it forever. By simplifying the process it will be easier to train new people to manage camps in the future.
